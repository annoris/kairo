Hey there! I'm Kairo Vance, a creative technologist based in Zurich
with a deep focus on spatial web engineering and kinetic interfaces.
My work lives at the intersection of real-time graphics, interaction
design, and high-craft frontend architecture.
When I'm not writing shaders or tuning spring physics, you'll find me
studying Swiss typography, industrial architecture, and generative art.
Every detail—from micro-timing to light response—shapes how a product feels.
Away from the screen, I recharge in the Swiss Alps—trail running,
analog photography, and exploring sound synthesis keep my perspective fresh.
I'm always excited to collaborate on ambitious digital experiences.
